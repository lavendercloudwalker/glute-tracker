How the estimates work

Ovulation usually happens 12 to 14 days before the next period. Until there are a few cycles logged, the app predicts from your average cycle length. Once you log a positive LH strip, it switches to that: ovulation is likely 24 to 36 hours after the first positive.

Fertile window: the 5 days before ovulation, ovulation day, and the day after as a buffer. Aim for intercourse every 1 to 2 days through it, especially the day of the positive LH and the day after.

LH strips: test once daily in the afternoon from the suggested start day, twice daily once the line starts darkening, until you get a positive.

See your GP if a cycle passes day 45 with no period and negative tests, or if cycles stay longer than 35 days.
